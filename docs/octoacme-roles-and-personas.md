# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction and help the team make sound implementation decisions. They bridge architecture, delivery, and quality concerns so the work remains feasible and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Guide solution design and technical trade-offs
- Review architecture, dependencies, and implementation approach
- Support estimation for complex work and technical spikes
- Escalate technical risks, constraints, and decisions early
- Partner with Developers and QA to keep solutions testable and supportable

### Goals
- Keep solutions aligned with long-term maintainability
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Improve delivery confidence for complex initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and implementation planning sessions
- Ongoing collaboration with Developers, PMs, and Product Managers
- Technical risk updates during delivery syncs

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the test strategy and help ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They coordinate validation efforts across the team and promote a shared understanding of quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and release validation approach
- Clarify acceptance criteria into testable scenarios
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Track defects, quality risks, and retest needs
- Work with Developers, PMs, and Product Managers to resolve quality gaps

### Goals
- Improve confidence in release readiness
- Catch defects early and reduce escaped issues
- Make quality expectations explicit and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect triage sessions
- Daily collaboration with Developers during verification
- Release readiness updates with PMs and stakeholders

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Leads facilitate the team’s workflow and help remove delivery blockers. They support predictable execution by improving ceremonies, flow, and follow-through.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify blockers and help coordinate resolution
- Keep work visible and support healthy team flow
- Reinforce agreed delivery practices and working agreements
- Coordinate dependencies and cross-team follow-up with the PM

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability
- Reduce friction in team execution
- Help the team stay focused on the highest-priority work

### Typical Communication
- Delivery ceremonies and status check-ins
- Dependency tracking with PMs and stakeholders
- Frequent coordination with Developers, QA, and Technical Leads

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business direction, approval, and support for priority decisions. They help ensure the work stays aligned to strategic goals and organizational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priorities and desired outcomes
- Approve major scope, timeline, or release decisions when needed
- Help resolve escalations and competing priorities
- Provide feedback on project progress and outcomes
- Support broader organizational alignment and adoption

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers business value
- Keep delivery aligned with organizational priorities
- Provide timely decision-making when trade-offs are required

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision requests and escalation discussions
- Review of major release or scope changes

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Representatives help ensure the project is ready for real-world use and that post-release support is effective. They bring operational perspective into planning, release readiness, and incident follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Review operational readiness, support impact, and handoff needs
- Prepare for release support, monitoring, and escalation paths
- Share incident trends or customer feedback that affect priorities
- Participate in post-release verification and support planning
- Contribute to retrospectives and continuous improvement actions

### Goals
- Reduce operational surprises after release
- Improve supportability and response readiness
- Strengthen feedback loops from production and customer support

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch planning
- Incident follow-up and support coordination
- Feedback sharing in retrospectives and stakeholder updates

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams can combine these personas to model realistic handoffs across planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.
